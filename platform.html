Platform Administrator

Creates and manages store accounts

This is a separate account The platform administrator is the account that creates a store and sets the store's own administrator login. The two are never the same account, and the platform administrator cannot open a store's books.

First run? The account is created automatically as platformadmin with the password in the PLATFORM_ADMIN_PASSWORD environment variable (default PlatformAdmin123!). Change it before going live.
