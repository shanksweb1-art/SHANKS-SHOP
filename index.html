About us

We build the till that Nigerian pharmacies actually wanted

SHANKS SHOP MANAGEMENT started in a real pharmacy, on a real counter, where the paper book was faster than the software. We rebuilt it around the way a busy store works: multi-package drugs, credit customers, cash that must balance at close of day, and expiry dates that quietly cost money.

  • Built with pharmacy owners in Nigeria, not copied from a generic retail POS.
  • Every stock movement is understood in base units, so a carton of 12 never quietly breaks your stock count.
  • Every price change, void, refund and login is recorded, so a dispute ends with facts.
