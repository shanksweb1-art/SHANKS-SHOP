Refund policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy covers refunds on platform subscriptions, refunds recorded inside the platform for your own customers, and what happens to your data if you leave.

The short version: try it for 14 days free. If it is not right for your store, tell us within 14 days of your first payment and we refund that month in full. After that, you can cancel any time and we refund any unused whole months.

1. The free trial

The first 14 days are free and no card details are taken. Nothing is charged until you choose a paid plan. If you never upgrade, there is nothing to refund and no obligation.

2. Refunds on the platform subscription

SituationWhat you get back
You tell us within 14 days of your first payment that the platform does not fit your store100% of the amount paid, returned to the original payment method within 7 working days
You cancel a monthly planThe current month runs to the end of the paid period. We do not refund the remainder of a month already in use
You cancel a yearly plan within 30 days of the renewalThe unused whole months of the year are refunded, less the months already used
You cancel a yearly plan after 30 daysNo refund on the current term, but you keep access to the end of the term and can export your data at any time
We withdraw a feature or change the platform in a way that materially harms your storeAt our cost, pro-rated refund of the remaining paid term

3. Plan upgrades and downgrades

  • Upgrade. Takes effect immediately. We charge the difference for the remainder of the current term, so nobody is charged twice for the same month.
  • Downgrade. Takes effect at the start of the next term. If the downgrade would leave you above the limits of the new plan, for example more tills than the plan allows, the change applies at the next renewal instead.
  • Change of billing cycle. Monthly to yearly is charged immediately at the yearly rate for the remaining term. Yearly to monthly starts at the next renewal.

4. How to ask for a refund

Contact us from the email address on the account, or through the chat on the home page, and tell us the store name and why. We will confirm the amount and the method within two working days. Refunds go back to the original payment method, so allow your bank or card issuer a few days to post it.

5. Refunds you record for your own customers

These are a commercial matter between your store and your customer, and the platform records them for you. A sale made in error can be voided, and a refund can be recorded against the original sale with a reason, so the stock and the audit trail stay correct. Note that voiding a sale returns the stock in base units, which matters when a product was sold in a carton or a pack.

6. What happens to your data if you leave

  • Take a full backup from the backups screen before you close the account. It is yours to keep.
  • After the paid term ends, the store is locked and the data is moved out of active systems.
  • Backups are retained for 30 days, then deleted. After that window the data can no longer be recovered by us.
  • Records that Nigerian tax, audit or pharmacy regulation requires us to keep are retained for the period the law requires, and are not used for any other purpose.

7. Payment problems

If a payment fails, we contact the store admin and the store keeps working for a grace period. We do not suspend a store's trading record for a failed payment, because the trading and audit history of a pharmacy should not disappear over a card error. Access to new features can be paused until the payment is settled.

8. Disputes

If you are not satisfied with a refund decision, reply to the decision and we will review it with the store's account record and audit trail. The audit log shows the plan changes, the payments and the cancellations, so the review is based on facts.
