Cookie policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains the cookies and local storage used by the SHANKS SHOP MANAGEMENT platform and by the marketing site. Cookies are small files a site stores on your device. Local storage works the same way but holds slightly larger values.

1. What we use on the platform

NameTypePurposeDuration
danzona_api_keyLocal storageIdentifies your store to the API so you stay signed inUntil you log out
danzona_usernameLocal storageIdentifies the staff account you are signed in asUntil you log out
danzona_current_userLocal storageCaches your name, role and store details for the interfaceUntil you log out
danzona_pharmacy_nameLocal storageShows your business name on receipts and the menuUntil you log out
danzona_business_typeLocal storageDecides which modules your store showsUntil you log out
salesCartLocal storageHolds the open cart so a refresh or a crash does not lose the saleUntil the sale is completed
session cookieCookieKeeps the session on the server so a request can be matched to a signed-in userExpires with the session

These are essential. Without them you cannot stay signed in and the till cannot keep an open cart, so they cannot be switched off.

2. What we use on the marketing site

NameTypePurposeDuration
shanks_themeLocal storageRemembers whether you chose the light or the dark themeUntil you clear it
shanks_cookie_choiceLocal storageRemembers your answer to the cookie notice so it is not shown againUntil you clear it

The home page also loads the Tawk.to live chat widget and the WhatsApp link, which are third-party services. They set their own cookies for the live chat, under their own policies. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Analytics

We do not run third-party advertising trackers or sell browsing data. Where we measure how the platform is used, we do it from the store's own records, which are already in the store's own isolated space.

4. Managing cookies

  • On the marketing site, use the cookie notice on your first visit to accept all cookies or keep only the essential ones, and clear the site data in your browser to reset that choice.
  • On the platform, the essential items listed above are needed to run the till. Signing out clears the store and user details from the device.
  • Every current browser also has a built-in way to block or delete cookies, in its privacy settings. Blocking essential storage will stop you signing in.

5. Changes

If we add a new cookie that is not essential, we will update this page and ask you again before it is set.
