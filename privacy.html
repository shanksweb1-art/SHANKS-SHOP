Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what SHANKS SHOP MANAGEMENT (the "platform") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It covers the stores that run their business on the platform, the staff accounts inside those stores, and the customers whose details a store records.

In one line: your store's data belongs to your store. We process it to run the platform for you, we do not sell it, and you can take a full backup or leave with it at any time.

1. Who we are

SHANKS SHOP MANAGEMENT is a point of sale and pharmacy management platform. The business that decides what is collected from its own customers is the store running on the platform. We provide the system, the hosting and the support.

2. What we collect

Account and store details

Business name, address, phone number, email address, store type, the modules the store switches on, and the staff accounts with their username, role and page access.

Trading data

Products and their packaging types, prices and cost prices, stock movements, sales, receipts, returns, expenses, purchases, payments, shifts and cash counts. This is the trading record of the store and it is created so the books can be kept.

Customer records held by a store

Where a store chooses to record a customer, it records the name, phone number, purchase history, loyalty points and any store-account balance. These records are created and used by the store, not by us.

Technical information

Your IP address, the browser and device you use, the pages you open and the actions you perform are recorded in the audit log, with the staff account that made them. This is a security feature: it is how a dispute about a price, a void or a stock adjustment gets settled with facts.

3. How we use it

  • To run the platform: process sales, hold stock, print receipts, produce reports and send the notifications a store turns on.
  • To bill the store for its plan and to support it.
  • To keep the platform secure, prevent abuse and investigate disputes.
  • To send the product newsletter, if and only if you ask for it. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who host the platform, deliver email or process payments, under a duty to keep it confidential and to use it only for us.
  • Your store's own staff, who can only see the parts of the platform their role allows.
  • Law enforcement or a regulator, where we are legally required to disclose.

5. Data you capture for your own customers

When a store records a customer's details in a prescription, a customer account or a delivery note, that store is the controller of that information. The platform stores it on the store's behalf, keeps it inside the store's own isolated space, and gives the store the tools to view, correct and delete it. A store's customers should direct requests about their own records to that store.

6. How long we keep it

Trading and audit records are kept for as long as the store's account is open, and for the period afterwards that Nigerian tax and pharmacy record-keeping rules require. Data of a store that closes and is deleted is removed from active systems after the backup retention window expires, which is stated in our refund policy.

7. Your rights

  • Access and correction. Any store admin can see and correct its products, customers, sales and stock inside the platform.
  • Export. A full database backup is available from the backups screen, at any time, in a portable format.
  • Deletion. Closing a store removes its data from the platform on the schedule in the refund policy.
  • Newsletter. You can unsubscribe from any email, or ask us to delete your email address entirely.

8. Cookies

The platform uses cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your store preferences. The marketing site uses a small amount of storage to remember your theme and cookie choice. Details are in the cookie policy.

9. Security

Data is stored in an isolated space per store, access is granted per staff role and per page, every meaningful action is written to the audit log, and databases are backed up on a schedule with tested restore. No system is perfect, so we keep the layers: least privilege, an audit trail and backups you can actually restore from.

10. Children

The platform is a business tool and is not directed at children. A store's customer records are collected for the purpose that store sets out, and should not include information about a child unless the law of the store's jurisdiction requires it.

11. Changes and contact

When this policy changes in a way that affects you, we update the date at the top of this page and tell store admins directly for material changes. For any privacy question, contact us using the details on the contact section of the home page, or write to info@shadepos.com.
